# Prompt 06 — Kiểm tra và sửa media

Audit all generated assets together. Reject and regenerate any asset with: malformed UAV; inconsistent sensor; impossible rotor/flight geometry; non-tropical forest; excessive sci-fi effects; visible text/logo; fake metrics; repeated composition; color mismatch; insufficient safe area; video flicker; subject morphing; broken loop.

Verify ratios, dimensions, naming, color consistency and safe areas. Confirm the video first and last frames loop smoothly. Create a contact sheet for visual comparison and an asset manifest containing filename, section, ratio, dimensions, format, alt-text suggestion and fallback. Do not approve assets merely because they are visually attractive; technical credibility and content safety take priority.
