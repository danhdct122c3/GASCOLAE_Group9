# Prompt 02 — Ảnh Hero

Create a premium photorealistic hero image for a Vietnamese forest carbon mapping service. An industrial multirotor UAV with a generic downward-facing LiDAR sensor flies above a dense Southeast Asian tropical forest in clear morning daylight. A subtle cyan and lime scan reveals a scientifically plausible 3D point cloud and terrain structure beneath part of the canopy.

Wide 16:9 composition. Place the UAV in the upper-right third and reserve the left 42 percent as calm negative space for Vietnamese headline and CTA. Bright, clean environmental technology aesthetic. Use pale mint, forest green #12735A, data blue #287A96 and restrained lime #9CF06A.

Avoid text, logos, brand names, futuristic interfaces, exaggerated beams, fantasy forests, inaccurate drone anatomy, agriculture spraying, charts, numbers and people. Export a 1920x1080 master plus 1600, 1280, 960 and 640px responsive variants. Also export a WebP poster matching the exact composition.
